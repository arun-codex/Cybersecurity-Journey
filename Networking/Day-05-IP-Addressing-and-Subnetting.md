## Learning objectives

- Understand IPv4 address structure, octets and binary representation.
- Distinguish private and public IPv4 addresses.
- Explain network and host portions of an IP address.
- Understand subnet masks and CIDR notation.
- Practise basic network, broadcast and usable host calculations.
- Connect subnetting to SOC investigations and network segmentation.

## Suggested study approach

The original Friday study slot was one hour. The playlist alone requires over two hours, so it can be split across sessions. Start with episodes 1–3, then continue with the remaining episodes and hands-on calculations at a comfortable pace. The expanded playlist is a learning resource, not a requirement to finish all advanced exercises in one day.
